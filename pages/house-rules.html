1. Rent & Payments
  • Rent is due on your monthly agreement date.
  • 3-day grace period is observed for settlements.
  • Digital payments (GCash/Maya/Bank) uploaded via portal.
2. Curfew & Quiet Hours
  • Gate locked at 10:00 PM for night security.
  • Late shift arrivals must notify caretaker in advance.
  • Quiet hours strictly enforced from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
3. Visitors & Guests
  • Visiting hours: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM in lounge only.
  • No unregistered guests inside bedrooms.
  • No overnight guests permitted.
4. Energy & Utilities
  • Turn off lights, fans, and faucets when exiting.
  • Laptops, phones, and personal fans are included.
  • Heavy appliances (aircon/induction) require approval.
5. Cleanliness & CLAYGO
  • Clean As You Go (CLAYGO) in dining and kitchen.
  • Do not leave unwashed utensils in sinks.
  • Properly segregate trash in designated bins.
6. Safety Restrictions
  • No smoking, vaping, or alcohol inside premises.
  • No unauthorized duplication of room/gate keys.
  • LAN network is strictly for registered boarders.
